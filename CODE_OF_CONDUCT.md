# Community standards

Fable2RE welcomes contributors interested in preservation, reverse engineering, native systems, tools, documentation, testing, and original mods. Participate with patience, technical honesty, and respect for other contributors and upstream projects.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and changes with evidence while treating people respectfully.
- State uncertainty, distinguish inference from observation, and correct mistakes openly.
- Credit prior work and preserve upstream authorship and license notices.
- Help newcomers work within the project's owned-copy and public-content boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or access when conduct undermines a safe and productive project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**, titled as a conduct report). Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy and respond proportionately.
